ABOUT

Brandon is an ESSA Accredited Exercise Scientist with experience in strength and conditioning, sport and athletic training and rehabilitation.

He works across multiple sports such as football, Australian Football League (AFL), badminton and general strength training, bringing an evidence-based approach to build athletes who can stay injury-free and perform at the highest level.

SERVICES

What Brandon does

01

Team Strength & Conditioning

Sport-specific programs built around your season, including pre-season preparation, in-season maintenance and off-season development.

02

School Athletic Development

Age-appropriate programs that teach young athletes to move well, lift safely and build physical literacy across all sports.

03

Speed, Agility & Power

Acceleration, change of direction and jump training to give athletes an edge on game day.

04

Injury Prevention & Return to Play

Warm-up protocols, injury risk reduction, taping and soft tissue support, and structured return-to-play programs after injury.

05

Athlete Testing & Monitoring

Fitness and performance testing, wellness and load tracking, and clear reports so coaches can see progress and make better decisions.
