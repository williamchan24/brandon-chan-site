ABOUT

Brandon is an ESSA Accredited Exercise Scientist in his third year of a Bachelor of Exercise and Sport Science at Swinburne University, with a real passion for injury management and prevention in sporting populations.

He brings a strong understanding of movement analysis, motor control and learning, athlete development and exercise physiology, with hands-on experience in strength and conditioning, sport and athletic training and rehabilitation across football, Australian Football League (AFL), soccer, badminton and general strength training.

Badminton is where his passion runs deepest. He uses evidence-based training and the principles of sport science to help players perform at their best while reducing their risk of injury.
